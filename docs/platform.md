# ILM Platform — getting started

This guide has moved into the user guide under [`docs/site/`](./site/overview.md), where it was
split across three pages. Installing the operator and its upstream prerequisites is
[Installation](./site/installation.md). The first-run walkthrough and the whole configuration
surface — prerequisite Secrets and their exact keys, external and managed infrastructure, the
edge, scaling, teardown — are [The Platform CR](./site/custom-resources/platform.md), with the
field-by-field index in [Platform options](./site/custom-resources/platform-options.md).
Watching a platform converge, reading its status conditions, and diagnosing one that will not
become ready are [Troubleshooting](./site/troubleshooting.md).

Those pages are published at https://docs.otilm.com.
